# Regulatory Directives

## RD-101: Release Reporting

1. Any release containing H2S, or reaching a watercourse, is immediately reportable within 1 hour.
2. Liquid release > 2.0 m³ is immediately reportable within 1 hour.
3. Liquid release ≤ 2.0 m³, fully contained, no watercourse/H2S/wildlife impact, is record-only (compliant).
4. Wildlife mortality is reportable within 24 hours.

## RD-114: Public Complaint Response

1. Every complaint receives acknowledgment within 2 business days.
2. Substantive response required within 20 business days.
3. Complaints describing health/life-safety risk escalate to duty officer within 1 hour; never closed without human decision.
4. Repeat contacts from same person/location are linked; response clock does not restart.

## RD-127: Proximity and Setback

1. Sour facilities maintain 500m setback from dwelling, 1500m from school/hospital/care facility.
2. Complaint from within setback zone of sour facility defaults to Tier 2+; only human review can lower it.

## RD-133: Flaring and Venting

1. Routine flaring limited to permitted volumes and hours.
2. Black smoke or off-hours flaring is reportable within 24 hours.
3. Repeated after-hours flaring within 30 days treated as single ongoing matter.

## RD-146: Site Access and Landowner Notification

1. Licensee gives 7+ days written notice before entering private land (non-emergency).
2. Entry without notice is a contravention; recorded against licensee.
3. Gates, fences, livestock controls restored to prior condition on exit.

## RD-158: Noise Control

1. 50 dBA (22:00–07:00), 60 dBA (07:00–22:00) at nearest receptor.
2. Complainant measurements indicative only; require qualified survey before contravention recorded.
