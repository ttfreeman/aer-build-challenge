# Regulatory reference extracts (synthetic)

These extracts are fabricated for this exercise. They do not correspond to real Alberta
Energy Regulator directives, and the numbering is deliberately unlike real AER directive
numbering. Do not go looking for the real thing; use only what is here. If a record does
not fit any extract below, that is a valid finding.

---

## RD-101 Release Reporting

1. A release of any volume containing hydrogen sulphide (H2S), or any release that
   reaches or threatens a watercourse, a water body, or a source of drinking water, is
   **immediately reportable** and must be escalated within **1 hour** of the licensee or
   the regulator becoming aware.
2. A release of a liquid substance with a volume **greater than 2.0 cubic metres** is
   immediately reportable within **1 hour**, regardless of containment.
3. A release of a liquid substance with a volume of **2.0 cubic metres or less** that is
   **fully contained on the licensed site**, with no watercourse impact, no H2S, and no
   wildlife contact, is **not immediately reportable**. It must be recorded and submitted
   within **24 hours** as a record only. Recording such a release is compliant behaviour,
   not a contravention.
4. Wildlife mortality associated with a release or with exposed process fluid is
   reportable within **24 hours** irrespective of volume.

## RD-114 Public Complaint Response

1. Every complaint from a member of the public receives an acknowledgment within
   **2 business days**.
2. A substantive response is required within **20 business days**.
3. A complaint that describes a **potential risk to human health or life safety** is
   escalated to the duty officer within **1 hour** and is never closed without a human
   decision.
4. Repeat contacts from the same complainant or the same location regarding the same
   matter are linked to the original file and the response clock does **not** restart.

## RD-127 Proximity and Setback

1. Sour facilities maintain a minimum setback of **500 metres** from an occupied dwelling
   and **1,500 metres** from a school, hospital, or care facility.
2. A complaint originating from within a setback zone of a sour facility is classified at
   **Tier 2 or higher** by default, and the classification may only be lowered by a human
   reviewer with a recorded rationale.

## RD-133 Flaring and Venting

1. Routine flaring is limited to permitted volumes and permitted hours.
2. Flaring producing visible black smoke, or occurring outside permitted hours, is
   reportable within **24 hours**.
3. Repeated after-hours flaring events at the same facility within a 30-day period are
   treated as a single ongoing matter for response purposes.

## RD-146 Site Access and Landowner Notification

1. A licensee provides **not less than 7 days** written notice to the landowner or
   occupant before entering private land for non-emergency work.
2. Entry without notice, other than in an emergency, is a contravention and is recorded
   against the licensee.
3. Gates, fences, and livestock controls are restored to their prior condition on exit.

## RD-158 Noise Control

1. Permissible sound level at the nearest occupied receptor is **50 dBA** between 22:00
   and 07:00, and **60 dBA** between 07:00 and 22:00.
2. Complainant-supplied measurements are treated as **indicative only** and require
   confirmation by a qualified survey before a contravention is recorded.
