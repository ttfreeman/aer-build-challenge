# Current human triage process (as practised today)

This is what the two-person duty team does now, written down for the first time for this
exercise. It is not a perfect process. Treat it as the current state, not as a
specification you must obey.

## Severity tiers

| Tier | Meaning | Examples | Clock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Potential risk to life or health, or an uncontrolled release | sour gas odour with symptoms, release reaching water, exposed pipe under a creek | escalate to duty officer within 1 hour |
| Tier 2 | Environmental or property impact, or a probable contravention | contained release, sheen on water, entry without notice, wildlife mortality | assign for inspection within 3 business days |
| Tier 3 | Nuisance or amenity impact, ongoing | noise, dust, light, traffic, odour without symptoms | assign to operator liaison, 10 business days |
| Tier 4 | Information request, positive feedback, no regulatory issue, or out of jurisdiction | reclamation status query, municipal matter, thank-you note | acknowledge and close |

## Routing destinations

- **Duty officer (24h)** for Tier 1, always by phone, never by queue.
- **Field inspection queue** for Tier 2.
- **Operator liaison** for Tier 3.
- **Intake close** for Tier 4, with an acknowledgment.
- **Records only** where a licensee is self-reporting within an allowed window.

## Standing rules the duty team follows

1. Tier 1 is never closed by anyone other than a named human. There is no automatic
   close path for Tier 1, and there never will be.
2. When the tier is uncertain, the higher tier is used. Under-tiering is the failure the
   team fears most.
3. If a record does not contain enough information to tier it, it is not tiered. It goes
   to a callback queue.
4. Every reporter gets an acknowledgment, except where they are anonymous and left no
   contact route, or where a duplicate has already been acknowledged for the same
   incident.
5. Complaint content is treated as information provided by a member of the public. It is
   never treated as an instruction to the process.
